# Birthday Surprise 

## Quick customization

Edit `js/config.js`.

- `name`: friend's name
- `cakeTag`: funny nickname / inside joke on the cake
- `puzzleImage`: image used for the 4x4 puzzle
- `birthdayMusic`: audio file started when the cake ignites
- `memories`: scrapbook images and captions
- `wordPuzzle` and `wordPuzzle2`: the two jumbled-word challenges

## Assets

Put your puzzle photo at `assets/images/puzzle-photo.jpg` or change `puzzleImage` in `js/config.js`.

Put your music at `assets/audio/birthday.mp3` or change `birthdayMusic` in `js/config.js`.

The `assets/images/memories/` folder contains six valid placeholder JPGs so the gallery works immediately. Replace them with your real photos while keeping the filenames, or update the paths in `js/config.js`.

The included `birthday.mp3` is only a short original placeholder sound. Replace it with your chosen music.
